The journey that began with The Machine Age and deepened with BioGenesis is now complete. The Veil has been pierced, and Shadows of the Shroud and the Stellaris 4.1 'Lyra' update are here!

Reality is only one thread in a vast, trembling web. Empires reach beyond the material realm, attuning to ancient powers and bargaining with entities that exist beyond time. Psionic Auras ripple outward, warping systems and reshaping destinies. While the Mindwardens wage silent war against psionic corruption, the Shroud Patrons gather, their gifts as lavish as their demands. The deeper one delves, the harder it is to return.

Shadows of the Shroud completes the 'Arc of Ascension', elevating Psionic Ascension to new heights with new interaction mechanics and a UI to track your exploration of the domains beyond. We also introduce a new path through the Shroud to forge your own path, refusing to make a covenant with eldritch entities.

The Stellaris Custodians continue to build upon the foundations of the 4.0 update with the 'Lyra' update. Significant improvements to the Planet UI are only one of their many steps forward - for more information, full release notes are available at the link below.
